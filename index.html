The connection that leaves the house with you

MJWIFI already gets underserved households online with a router and an "unlimited" data SIM — ~8,000 households today, growing ~10% month-on-month through 35 field agents. But the signal stops at the front door, and when whoever's holding the router steps out, the whole household drops offline with them. MJWIFI Mobile closes that gap: a personal SIM for every household member, sold by the same agents on the same visit, that pays back mobile data and a matched Data Vault boost every time it's topped up.

One router, one point of failure. One SIM each — always connected.

The Opportunity

A router connects the house. A phone goes everywhere.

MJWIFI already promises customers connectivity "whether at home or on the go" — but a single shared router can only deliver half of that. The moment someone steps outside, or the router-holder leaves with it, everyone else in the household is offline. A phone is in hand every waking hour, everywhere the router can't reach. An MJWIFI Mobile SIM turns that promise into reality: MJWIFI becomes a network people carry, not just a box in the lounge — on infrastructure MJWIFI already owns: the field-agent relationship, the household trust, and the brand.

The flywheel: every top-up earns data-back + a Data Vault boost — which drives more frequent top-ups, deeper household trust and word-of-mouth referrals — the recurring revenue a growing connectivity provider needs beyond the one-time router sale.

02 · Right to Win

Why MJWIFI wins in mobile

This is not a me-too MVNO. MJWIFI brings four structural advantages no telco or reseller can replicate.

📡 The connectivity rails already exist

MJWIFI already runs the router install base, the top-up rails and device security partnerships, with 35 field agents already inside the home. Mobile plugs into a live connectivity spine for billing, top-ups and rewards — not a greenfield build.

🔋 Rewards that become reach

Only MJWIFI can route mobile rewards straight into a growing Data Vault and bank spare data instead of losing it — turning a personal SIM into the top-ups, usage and engagement a growing household-router network actually needs.

📱 App + field-agent distribution

MJWIFI's 35 field agents already sell and install the router door-to-door in underserved communities. SIM sign-up, RICA and self-care nest inside a channel that already acquires household-by-household — near-zero incremental CAC.

🤝 Trust, and no price war

Households already trust MJWIFI enough to let an agent inside and install a router. Reward-funded data lowers the effective price without a tariff war — and a personal SIM billed to the same account is the strongest retention lock a growing ISP can hold.
